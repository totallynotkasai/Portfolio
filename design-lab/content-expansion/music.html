Keyboard: arrows move the cursor; Enter draws a fresh attack; Delete erases. Split/Join acts on the cursor step. On a phone, use the step-page buttons to move through time.

Try these three gestures

Draw C4 across steps 1–4 for one held note. Clear, then tap C4 at steps 1 and 3 for two attacks with a rest. Load the sample, enable its overlay, and change a pitch.

Prototype limits

One 16-step bar, C major, one pitch per step and a simple sine voice. No named saves, reload persistence, longer patterns, import/export or full Match the tune activity yet. Sound is local to this preview; it does not change the portfolio sound preference. Phone listening and touch comfort await your review.
