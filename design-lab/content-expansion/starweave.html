An unwritten spell

Try making a spell

Load Wizard’s lullaby and cast it. Replace a moon with an ember and listen to the change. Move outward for a higher voice; let a moon ring for four moments. The wizard conducts each sounded rune and gets excited when you combine all three ingredients.

Keyboard on a numbered moment: Left/Right chooses time, Up/Down changes the selected pitch, Enter places your rune and Delete erases. The palette and pitch buttons provide the same controls on a phone.
