Drag here to move your hat

Tap/drag the arena in Move or Aim mode. The lower pad always moves. Aim with the slider or Q/E, then Cast. Keyboard: arrows/WASD move, J casts, Space/K shields, P pauses.

My hat versus yours. No hard feelings?

Protect two things

Hat hit: −1 heart.
Portal hit: −2 hearts.
Dodging may save your hat but expose your portal. A timed shield sends the spell back along your aim.

Make the shot magical

△ Shoot through Fire: faster travel and +1 damage to either target.

ϟ Shoot through Electric: next two side banks turn 45°, still travelling toward the same end.

Shield with timing

A 0.35s parry window, then 1.8s recharge. One successful parry spends the shield. Holding the button doesn’t keep it up. Runes stay on a parried shot.

Practice & review controls

Turn the pet off for a quiet arena. Practice sends a plain shot at your current position so you can rehearse shielding. It still causes normal damage.

What to try

Move off-centre and aim a shot through a rune. Stay near your portal when a spell comes back. Try dodging one shot and parrying the next. The wizard visibly charges before casting and reacts to your parries and hits.

Prototype limits

Initial damage, shield timing and AI tuning; one cast per side, eight-second spell lifetime, no orb-to-orb collisions. Hats use circular hit areas under their drawings. No saved records, production sound, progression or desktop integration yet. Real-phone controls and the balance between defending yourself and the portal await your feedback.
