Compare the powers

○ Plain

Ordinary wall banks and bounded striker impulses. Try this first.

F · Fire

Five active seconds. Side banks add 10% speed (up to 1.3× normal). Next striker hit adds a stronger return (up to 1.4×) and a small recoil, then consumes Fire. Recoil keeps your controls active.

E · Electric

Six active seconds or two side banks. Charged banks leave at 45°, continuing toward the same end. The dashed guide predicts that bank. Next striker hit adds 25% more sideways contribution, then consumes Electric.

Pickups

The puck collects them. First after 8 active seconds, then 10–14 seconds apart. A dotted outline warms up for 0.6s; uncollected pickups expire after 6s. No stacking.

Prototype limits & review

Starting physics and AI tuning, one table, no saved statistics, no production sound or wizard integration. Scoring, goals, rounded posts and moving-striker sweeps are implemented; extreme repeated contacts need more playtesting before integration. Keyboard controls work, but this real-time game has no equivalent nonvisual mode. Actual phone gestures and rally/power feel await your review.

Try a plain rally, then Fire's recoil and Electric's bank angle. Does either make a return feel unfair? Does the control pad keep the puck easy to see? Powers and pickup timers freeze on pause; switching tabs/windows pauses play and requires Resume.

Review only. Resize changes the projection, not table physics. New match alternates the first serve; after a goal the conceding side receives.
